# Code of conduct

Synchronic is about history that people feel strongly about: their countries, their families, wars and revolutions that are still argued over. We want everyone, whatever their nationality, language, religion, politics or background, to be able to work on it without being attacked for who they are.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and anything posted on the project's behalf.

## What we expect

- Be respectful of people, even when you think their claim is wrong.
- Argue with sources, not with nationality or identity. A correction is about the data, never about the person who wrote it.
- Assume good faith, especially across languages. Many contributors write in their second or third language.
- Accept that disputes are settled by the [content policy](CONTRIBUTING.md#content-policy), and that a maintainer's decision under it is not a personal judgement.
- Give and take review graciously.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Slurs, or demeaning remarks about nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or any other part of someone's identity.
- Denial or glorification of genocide, mass atrocities or crimes against humanity, or hate propaganda presented as "the other side".
- Deliberately falsifying data or sources, or edit wars instead of discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers: **[contact to be added before launch]**. Do not report them in a public issue.

Every report is read and kept confidential. Reports are handled by a maintainer who is not involved; if a maintainer is the subject, another maintainer handles it.

## What happens

Maintainers may, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is:

1. Ask the person, privately, to stop, and explain why.
2. Issue a public or private warning.
3. Remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code.
4. Ban the person from the project for a time, or permanently.
