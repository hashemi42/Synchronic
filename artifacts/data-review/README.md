Source URLs: 0 of 1348 do not resolve; 0 cited for a person do not mention them.

Terms Wikidata lacks: 89 of 89 are `source-confirmed` (a cited page states the years as a range, or a single-year term's year next to the name) and moved to *low*. 0 more have both years somewhere on the page but not as a range; 0 have neither. Those 0 are left for a person to check.

Persian names that differ from Wikidata or have no Persian label are also in [persian-names.csv](persian-names.csv) (104 rows), with a proposal under the naming rule (states: official name; people: short common name). 5 of them would change.

33 Wikidata holders the data leaves out on purpose are not listed; each has its rule in [data/policy/audit-exceptions.json](../../data/policy/audit-exceptions.json).

**Severity.** *high*: a source that fails, or years more than one apart. *medium*: a one-year difference, a record Wikidata lacks or has and we do not, a Persian name that differs or has no Wikidata label. *low*: Wikidata lacks a date, or models the office differently.

Wikidata is a cross-check, not the truth: where it disagrees, the cited source decides.
